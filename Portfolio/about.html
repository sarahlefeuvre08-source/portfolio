01 — My Story

Hi, I'm Sarah Lefeuvre — a UX/UI Designer with a background in beauty and a big love for creative, human-centered projects.

My journey into design started unexpectedly after a conversation with a designer at a party sparked my curiosity. The blend of empathy, problem-solving and creativity immediately felt like the perfect fit, and I haven't looked back since.

I'm now focused on creating intuitive and engaging digital experiences that make people's lives easier — and a little brighter.
